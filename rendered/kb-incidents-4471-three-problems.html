The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so their dates come out with day and month swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. It reaches people through the app, by email and through booking-v2 forms. This is the bug the queued fix is for.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first. Its date field shows MM/DD/YYYY, so people type the month first, and the form reads the date the tenant's way, day first. The date flips before the parser sees it. Only reports that come in through a booking-v3 form belong here. Fixing the parser does not fix the hint, so these keep coming after #4471's fix ships.

one-day move: a date lands one day off, not flipped. "Tuesday 12 May" shows as "Monday 11 May". A client in Lisbon and a client in Toronto see different days for the same meeting. An invite arrives before the booking was made. Nothing is swapped, so the parser fix will not touch it. It looks like a time zone conversion. These are the reports the desk already read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) and still filed under parser by tenant.
